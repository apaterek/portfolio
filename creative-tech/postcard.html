Postcard A multi-agent documentary production system

The future of content for experiences.

Postcard is an agentic production model for the experience economy. It makes short-form documentary content for guests at theme parks, resorts, cruises, and immersive attractions, and it does so without ever needing a phone or a camera to come between the customer and the thing they came for. The designed environment is treated as a character rather than a backdrop: the space makes demands, the subject responds, and the film documents that exchange.

The system is named for Jean Rouch, the founder of cinéma vérité, who described his filmmaking as "the postcard at the service of the imaginary." That is the governing premise, and it is a working constraint rather than a piece of branding. Documentary is not an objective document. The camera does not capture reality, it provokes and reshapes it, so every agent in the pipeline is written to understand that its task is not transcription but transformation through observation.

The architecture runs on three planes. An oversight plane carries an executive agent for coordination and brand fit, an ethics review agent for standards and alerts, and a recursive tone evaluation that runs back across every stage rather than only at the end. A production plane moves from ingest and organization through footage analysis, story edit, picture edit, sound edit, and online conform. A data plane holds the RAG index, stock and music libraries, proxies, and masters, and is swappable per project.

Under the footage analysis sits the part I find most interesting and most dangerous: transcription paired with valence, arousal, and dominance scoring drawn from the NRC VAD lexicon. Reading affect by machine is exactly the kind of capability that is easy to deploy and hard to deploy responsibly, which is why the ethics literature on emotion recognition sits in the research alongside the lexicon itself, and why the ethics review agent is a peer of the executive agent rather than a step at the end.

The work to date is a designed system rather than a running one: a framework document at Draft 1.1 covering foundational philosophy, aesthetic principles, intellectual lineage, and a written constitution for each agent, plus the architecture diagrams and the research corpus behind them. The framework carries a section titled "What Remains to Be Written," and the ethics constitution is the one still marked for expansion. That ordering is deliberate.

As sole author, I wrote the ideology and system framework and designed the agent architecture, the oversight model, and the data plane behind it.
