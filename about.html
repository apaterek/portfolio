About

I'm a creative strategist and producer building for audiences.

I came to this through anthropology, which trained me to watch what people actually do instead of what a deck promises they will. That lens still runs everything: documentary, branded entertainment, original formats, and increasingly work made to be entered rather than watched. My work sits where brand ambition meets culture, and the question underneath it never changes. What does an audience do with a story once it has their attention?

Across work for Google Arts & Culture, Honda, Nutella, AXS, The Pokémon Company, and YouTube, the throughline holds. Find the honest human story inside a brief, the behavior sitting underneath it, and the opening where a brand can take part instead of interrupt. Then carry that from observation into something real: concept and writing, casting, sitting with directors and editors, keeping agency and client teams pointed the same direction, and holding several productions at once to one tone. I've worked as a writer, a creative producer, and a format creator, often as part of a team, and I try to name what I actually owned.

Lately the same instinct pulls past the screen and into physical space, where research, concept, and production have to agree on what a person will actually walk through. I build to think, using creative coding, generative workflows, and small interactive prototypes to test an idea against real behavior before a production commits to it. The tools are just another material, useful to the degree they make an experience clearer.

I also write criticism about play, aesthetics, and the culture this work draws from. It isn't a side interest, it's the same research my graduate work in visual anthropology started. How people play, what they carry out of a room, and why attention holds in some places and leaks in others are the questions that decide whether an experience is worth standing in.

Current focus Experience strategy and producing for live entertainment, museums, and location-based storytelling, with creative technology and interactive work in the mix
Based in Los Angeles, California
