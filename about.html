About

I'm a creative strategist and producer building for audiences.

I came to this through visual anthropology, which trained me to watch what people do instead of what a deck promises they will. That lens still runs everything I do: content strategy, development, production, and increasingly working in built and experiential spaces. My work sits where brand ambition meets culture, and the question underneath it never changes. What does an audience do with a story once it has their attention? For me, it's always about a consistent process: understanding culture, crafting an experience, and imagining what comes next.

Across two decades of work for Google Arts & Culture, Honda, Nutella, AXS, The Pokémon Company, YouTube, and other clients, the throughline holds. Find the honest human story inside a brief, the behavior sitting underneath it, and the opening where a brand can take part. Then carry that from observation into something real: concept and writing, casting, keeping agency and client teams pointed the same direction, and ensuring production workflows deliver to all stakeholders on time and on budget. I've worked as a writer, a creative producer, and a format creator, often as part of a team, and I try to name what I actually owned.

Lately the same instinct is pulling me beyond screens and into physical spaces, where research, concept, and production have to agree on what a person will actually experience. With graduate training in visual anthropology, I approach creative strategy through research as much as storytelling, looking for the cultural patterns, behaviors, and motivations that shape memorable experiences.

I also write criticism about play, aesthetics, and the culture this work draws from. It isn't a side interest; it's the same research my graduate work in visual anthropology started. How people play, what they carry out of a room, and why attention holds in some places and leaks in others are the questions that decide whether an experience is worth our time.

Current focus Experience strategy and producing for live entertainment, museums, and location-based storytelling, with creative technology and interactive work in the mix
Based in Los Angeles, California
