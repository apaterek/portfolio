Off The Plate Original Format & Brand

Off The Plate is an original streaming format: a travel series exploring the culinary and cultural landscape surrounding Major League ballparks, international baseball landmarks, and the communities that feed them. Created independently, built from the outset to travel as a format and as a brand.

The first document is the series pitch deck, honed to meet market demands and tailored to major streamers with extreme interest. The second is a letter. It is copywriting, and it was also part of a vital outreach process to garner buy-in from one of many stakeholders. A series like this requires participation from MLB, MLB Studios, and all 30 Major League franchises, just for starters, so strong, honest, passionate creative that paints a clear and exciting picture is a must.

  • Series pitch deck (PDF) Format, positioning, and episode architecture, tailored to a major streamer.
  • Stakeholder outreach letter (PDF) Copywriting in service of buy-in: a direct approach to a Major League franchise.
