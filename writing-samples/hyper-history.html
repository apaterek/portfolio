Hyper History Google Arts & Culture with Vsauce2

A series made for the launch of Google Arts & Culture's natural history collection, produced with the YouTube channel Vsauce2. Rather than tour an archive of 50+ institutions, each episode pulled one object out of it (Lucy, the last passenger pigeon) and told the strange human story attached to it.

The writing brief had three things to hold at once: the launch marketing for Google Arts & Culture, the spirit and tone of Kevin, the narrator of Vsauce2, and a style comp both agency and client liked, the short film "A Brief History of John Baldessari," narrated by Tom Waits and set to the William Tell Overture.

The samples below are final drafts. They came out of a process of producing three drafts of episode scripts per day while preparing archival and creative for an involved, asset-heavy editorial process: writing to the edit, keeping every stakeholder aligned, and making sure what we created was true to the comp but wholly original, all on an expedited timeline.
