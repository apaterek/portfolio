Tryouts Competition Format Pitch Deck

Tryouts is a competition series pitting aspiring sports broadcasters (former athletes, influencers, and journalists) against each other in challenges built around storytelling, not just sports knowledge, to find the next great voice in sports media.

The format was designed to leverage the massive surge in sports-related content and the platform demand sitting behind it: live, streaming-first programming that can grow a subscriber base and hold it. The finale is live by design, and the challenges are built around the formats sports audiences already follow, from live game calls and studio punditry to podcasting and short-form video.

The deck maps the full format: mini and main challenges, judging, a live-sports finale, and the pitch-to-network grand prize. Registered with the WGA and shared here as a format and copywriting sample.
