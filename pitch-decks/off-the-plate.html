Off The Plate Series Pitch Deck

Off The Plate is an original travel format: actor and host Andrew J. West visits Major League ballparks around the world, chasing the food, culture, and people that surround the game. The deck was built for major streamers, with Amber Stevens West attached as executive producer.

As co-creator and writer, I built the series architecture end to end: the eight-city Season One route, sample episode outlines, comps, and the pitch language itself. It's registered with the WGA and shared here as a copywriting and format-development sample, not a produced series.
