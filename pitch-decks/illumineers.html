The Illumineers Lifestyle Format Pitch Deck

The Illumineers pairs Disney's Lorcana trading card game with the history and lore of Disney animation itself. Each episode, host Amber Stevens West and a guest travel to a Disney animation landmark, meet the artists who call themselves lorekeepers, then close the episode with a celebrity card match.

Written and developed to pitch alongside Lorcana's 2023 launch, the deck covers format, casting, and the consumer-products case for Disney+ and shopDisney. Shared here as a sample of format writing and presentation design for a licensed-IP pitch.
